How this page works

Compares what each rule finds on Flickr with the album that exists there today. The check itself changes nothing.

A rule is safe when turning on auto-sync would only add photos or leave the album as it is. Rules that would remove photos, or whose search hit Flickr's limit, are not safe and need a manual look first. Rules sorted newest first are the exception: they are searched year by year and keep the newest 4500 photos, so the album is capped on purpose.
