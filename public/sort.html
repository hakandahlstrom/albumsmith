How this page works

Flickr keeps your albums in a custom order. Here you can put the whole list in order by title, by when the album was last updated, or by number of photos.

Choose an order and click Show new order to see the result. Nothing changes on Flickr until you click Apply to Flickr. Only the order of the album list changes. No photos or albums are touched.

An album counts as updated when photos are added to or removed from it. Collections are not handled yet.
